# Freetally Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 15, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Freetally is an app designed to help you keep track of your freelance projects: client or
platform, agreed payment, hours worked, project status (in progress, delivered, paid,
pending testing) and important dates. This policy explains, clearly and without fine print,
what happens to the information you enter into the app.

The core idea is simple: **your data belongs to you, and it stays on your phone.**

## 2. What data is stored, and where

All the information you enter in Freetally — project names, clients or platforms, amounts,
hours, statuses and dates — is stored **only in your device's local storage**.

There is no Freetally server. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. No one
other than you (and anyone with physical access to your device) can see that data, because it
never leaves your device.

## 3. What we do NOT do

To be explicit, Freetally:

- **Does not send your data over the internet.** The whole app works offline. The only
  exception is asking Google Play whether you bought the premium unlock (see section 7), and
  no data about your projects travels in that check.
- **Does not include analytics or tracking** of your activity within the app.
- **Does not show advertising** or share data with ad networks.
- **Does not share or send your data to third parties**, nor to the app's developer.
- **Does not use Android's automatic backup.** Android can upload app data to your Google
  Drive on its own; in Freetally that feature is **deliberately switched off**, so your
  projects never leave the device by that route either. The trade-off is that your data is not
  restored automatically when you change phones: use the manual backup in Settings to take it
  with you.

## 4. Local notifications

Freetally may show you reminders (for example, about delivery dates, payment dates, or
pending test reviews). These notifications are generated **directly on your device**, without
going through any external push-notification server or sending any of your data to anyone.

## 5. Fingerprint or PIN lock

Freetally offers, as an **optional feature disabled by default**, the ability to lock access
to the app with your fingerprint or a PIN.

- If you use fingerprint authentication, it is **your device's own operating system** that
  processes and verifies it. Freetally **never receives or stores any biometric data**: it
  only receives a "yes" or "no" confirmation of whether authentication succeeded.
- If you use a PIN as a backup method, it is stored **encrypted (as a hash)** on your own
  device, never in plain text, and it never leaves your device.

## 6. Backup and data export

Freetally includes an optional backup feature (in JSON format), **free for all users**, and an
export of your income in CSV format, available as part of the **paid (premium) version**.

This feature is always under your control: you decide when to generate the file, and through
Android's own share system, you choose where to save it or who to send it to (for example,
your email, a personal cloud storage, or another app). Freetally does not send that file
anywhere on its own.

## 7. In-app purchases / Google Play Billing

Unlocking the premium version is done through a one-time purchase managed via **Google Play
Billing**. Google securely processes the payment; Freetally only receives confirmation that
the purchase was completed successfully. The app **does not handle or store any card or
payment data**.

The app asks Google Play whether this account already bought the unlock in only three moments:
when you open the app, when you open the purchase screen (in both cases so your unlock is
restored automatically if you switch phones or reinstall), and when you tap "Restore purchase".
No data about your projects travels in that check. Outside those three cases, normal use of the
app — creating projects, viewing totals, reminders — makes no connection at all.

## 8. Children

Freetally is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from
children.

## 9. Your rights / how to delete your data

Since all your data lives exclusively on your device, you are always in full control of it:

- You can delete any specific project or data directly from within the app.
- You can uninstall the app at any time, which completely removes all stored data.

There are no copies of your data anywhere else that need to be deleted, because it never left
your device.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future to reflect changes to the app. Any changes
will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
